# 🎤 Presentation Bookings

**No more "sorry, can I swap with someone?" emails.**
A little web app where students grab a slot to present their work, and the lecturer gets a tidy timetable without lifting a spreadsheet.

## ✨ What it does

### For students 🎓
- Sign in with just your student number (`ST` + 8 digits, e.g. `ST12345678`). No passwords to forget.
- See every upcoming presentation day: the date, time, venue and any notes from your lecturer.
- Pick an open slot, add your name and project title, and you're booked.
- Changed your mind? Cancel and pick another one.
- Taken slots just say **Booked**. Nobody can see who you are except the lecturer.

### For the lecturer 🧑‍🏫
- Sign in with your PIN and land on your own page.
- Open a presentation day in one go: date, start and end time, how long each presentation is, the changeover between them (5 minutes by default) and the venue.
- Add **tea or lunch breaks**. The slots politely step around them.
- Watch bookings roll in **live**, with each student's name, number and project title.
- Remove a booking, change the venue, delete a day, or copy the whole schedule to paste into an email.

### Behind the scenes 🛡️
- **No double-booking.** Two students can't grab the same slot, even if they click at the exact same second.
- **One slot per student per day.**
- **Private details stay private.** Names and student numbers are only readable by the lecturer and the student who booked.
- **Everything updates live** for everyone, no refresh button needed.
